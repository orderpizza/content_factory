# Product goal

Build a repeatable, profitable AI-assisted social content operation, starting
with one Instagram account. Expand to differentiated accounts and other platforms
only after production and measurement demonstrate value.

## Business hypotheses

A focused audience may return for useful, recognizable content; AI may reduce
production time enough to make consistent publishing economical. Sponsorship,
affiliate offers and owned products are possible revenue models, not validated
revenue streams. Select an initial audience, offer and acquisition channel before
optimizing volume. Existing English, AI/Tech and Psychology domains are current
editorial capabilities, not evidence of market demand or three operating accounts.

## MVP measures

Before a pilot, agree a time window, baseline and numeric targets for one account.
No production outcome has been established by the review-only pipeline.

| Outcome | Measure and decision |
| --- | --- |
| Useful content | Human acceptance rate, factual corrections and revision rounds per approved post; reject unsafe or inaccurate output. |
| Repeatable operation | Approved posts delivered per week, time from idea to review/publication, operator minutes per post, failed and uncertain deliveries. |
| Audience response | Platform-reported reach, saves, shares and follows per published post, with collection time and denominator. |
| Unit economics | Model/image spend plus operator time and delivery costs per approved/published post; attributed revenue less operating costs over the pilot. |
| Safe operation | Every publication has explicit authorization and exact reviewed assets; uncertain sends are reconciled before retry. |

Record missing data as unknown, not zero. Compare cohorts with the same observation
window; engagement is not revenue. Continue only when quality, sustainable effort
and an explicit economic hypothesis justify the next investment.
