# Historical acceptance reports

These reports and selected executable evidence preserve findings and
recommendations made during their original acceptance work. They are not current
architecture, active regression definitions, or runtime requirements; see the
[acceptance architecture](../README.md) and [current status](../current-status.md).

They are retained temporarily for investigation provenance and may be removed
once their findings are fully incorporated into permanent tests or specs. Git
history remains the recovery path after removal. The retained Psychology files
in `pass2/cases/` are historical, excluded from normal acceptance
profiles, and candidates for eventual deletion.
